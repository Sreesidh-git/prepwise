🔍 How to Report an Incorrect Question or Solution (Errata)

High editorial accuracy is central to student trust. While our questions undergo automated and mathematical verification, recruitment patterns and quantitative nuances change. If you spot a mathematical calculation error, an ambiguous question premise, or an outdated test pattern, please help us fix it:

  1. Note the details: Mention the Company (e.g., TCS, Infosys, Amazon), Category (e.g., Quantitative Aptitude), and the Question Text or number.
  2. Describe the issue: Briefly explain why the current answer or explanation is disputed (e.g., calculation discrepancy, missing option).
  3. Send an email: Email us at ra416491@gmail.com with the subject [Question Errata] Company - Topic.

Editorial Review Workflow: Once reported, our editorial team tags the question for review in our database. Flagged questions display an advisory banner to students until the recalculation is validated and republished.

Organizational Transparency

PrepWise is an independent digital educational resource. We do not operate commercial brick-and-mortar coaching centers, paid placement consultancy services, or commercial telemarketing operations.

We will never call, WhatsApp, or email students demanding payment for question access, certificates, or guaranteed job interviews. If you receive any solicitations claiming to be from PrepWise charging fees, please report them immediately.

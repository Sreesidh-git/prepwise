Why We Built PrepWise

Every placement season, millions of engineering and science students waste hours navigating clunky, outdated websites. They hit forced registration gates, aggressive paywalls, and spammy popups just to practice 10 aptitude questions or find out what a company's test pattern actually looks like.

PrepWise fixes this. We believe high-quality interview preparation should be as accessible as reading Wikipedia. You click on a company, see their recruitment patterns, and instantly start practicing high-yield MCQs with step-by-step explanations.

Our Core Principles

  • ✨
    Zero Friction: No signups, email verifications, passwords, or paywalls. Ever.
  • 🎯
    Company-Centric Focus: Realistic difficulty and question distributions mirroring actual hiring tests (TCS NQT, Infosys InfyTQ, Amazon SDE, Google, etc.).
  • 🧠
    Step-by-Step Solutions: Don't just memorize the correct letter. Understand the exact problem-solving methodology with breakdown steps.
  • ⚖️
    Sustainable & Respectful Ad Placements: Monetized solely through clean, non-intrusive standard display banners. No popups or redirects that break study focus.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is PrepWise really 100% free?

Yes. All company profiles, test blueprints, and 500+ questions with step-by-step solutions are completely free for all students worldwide.

How are the questions curated?

Our questions are crafted and calibrated based on recent recruitment patterns, syllabus specifications, and feedback from candidates who appeared in campus placement drives.

Can I practice on mobile?

Absolutely. PrepWise is built with a responsive, mobile-first design so you can practice on the bus, in class, or at your desk.

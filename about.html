Why We Built PrepWise

Every placement season, millions of engineering and science students waste hours navigating clunky, outdated websites. They hit forced registration gates, aggressive paywalls, spammy popups, and email harvesting forms just to practice 10 aptitude questions or find out what a company's recruitment pattern actually looks like.

PrepWise fixes this. We believe high-quality interview preparation should be as accessible as reading Wikipedia. You click on a company, see their recruitment patterns, and instantly start practicing high-yield MCQs with step-by-step explanations.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is PrepWise really 100% free?

Yes. All company profiles, test blueprints, and 1,100+ questions with step-by-step solutions are completely free for all students worldwide.

How are the questions curated?

Our questions are independently crafted, mathematically verified, and calibrated based on recent recruitment patterns, syllabus specifications, and feedback from candidates who appeared in campus placement drives. They are educational practice problems, not leaked or official test papers.

What if I find an incorrect answer or typo?

We maintain an active editorial review registry. Visit our Contact & Errata page to report the question ID, and our team will verify, flag, and update the question.

Can I practice on mobile?

Absolutely. PrepWise is built with a responsive, mobile-first design so you can practice on mobile (down to 360px), tablet, or desktop.

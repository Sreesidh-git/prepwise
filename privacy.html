PrepWise Privacy Policy

We believe studying should not require surrendering your personal data.
Learn how PrepWise operates without visitor signups, passwords, or invasive tracking cookies.

Last Updated: November 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Our Core Privacy Commitment

PrepWise is built on a zero-login, zero-friction philosophy. We do not require students, candidates, or job seekers to create an account, verify an email address, link social media profiles, or provide phone numbers to access practice questions, test patterns, or company syllabus guides.

Because we do not ask for or store Personally Identifiable Information (PII) from public visitors, your identity, contact information, and study history remain entirely your own.

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

To maintain platform performance, measure question utility, and monitor website reliability, PrepWise collects minimal, anonymized first-party telemetry through our serverless edge functions:

  • Page Navigation Telemetry: Anonymized event payloads recording the URL path visited, device category (desktop, tablet, mobile), generic screen width, and approximate referral source.
  • Company & Category Engagement: Records of which company recruitment papers (e.g., TCS, Infosys, Amazon) and aptitude modules are viewed to identify high-demand placement seasons.
  • Quiz Performance Aggregation: Anonymized quiz start and completion events, score tallies, and question counts used solely to evaluate question difficulty curves and identify questions that students consistently find challenging.
  • Search Length Telemetry (PII Protection): When you search for companies or roles in our search bar, our system logs only the character length of the query (e.g. queryLength: 6). Raw search keywords and text inputs are never stored in our analytics database to prevent accidental entry of sensitive personal details.

3. Cookies and Local Browser Storage

PrepWise distinguishes strictly between essential functional storage and optional telemetry:

A. Strictly Necessary Storage (Always Active)

These items are essential for core platform operation and security. They do not track your activity across other websites:

  • prepwise_consent_preferences (localStorage): Remembers your privacy and cookie choices so you are not re-prompted on every visit.
  • prepwise_in_progress_quiz (sessionStorage): Temporarily stores question progress during an active practice test so selections are not lost on accidental page refresh. Discarded upon completion.
  • Security & CSRF Headers: Serverless session verification to safeguard feedback submissions and administrative controls.

B. Optional Analytics Telemetry (Gated by Consent)

Optional telemetry is strictly blocked unless you grant permission through our consent interface:

  • prepwise_anonymous_id (localStorage): A randomized UUID used to aggregate unique visitor counts. Contains no personal information, email, or device serials.
  • prepwise_session_id (localStorage): A randomized identifier marking the start and end of a study session.
  • prepwise_analytics_events (sessionStorage): An ephemeral hash map used to debounce duplicate page-view events within a 24-hour study period.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4): If enabled in deployment configuration, basic anonymized visit counts are recorded with IP anonymization. Blocked until analytics consent is given.

C. Advertising Storage

Currently, zero advertising cookies or third-party ad storage are set on your device because all 13 advertising placements are disabled.

4. Advertising and Monetization Disclosures

Monetization with Adsterra: To sustain free, unpaywalled interview preparation access for students globally, PrepWise displays standard, non-intrusive advertisements from Adsterra across three dedicated content placements:

  • The Three Active Display Placements: (1) Homepage in-content break (728×90 desktop / 320×50 mobile), (2) Company profile role benchmarks break (native widget desktop / 300×250 mobile), and (3) Post-quiz completion results banner (728×90 desktop / 300×250 mobile).
  • Intrusive Formats Permanently Prohibited: Zero popunders, onclick popups, floating notification bars (Social Bar), full-screen interstitials, forced redirects, or smartlinks run on this website. All ad technology is strictly restricted to container display banners.
  • Zero In-Quiz Interruptions: Advertisements never appear within questions, between answer choices, or during active timed quizzes. The results banner appears strictly after a practice quiz is completed and submitted.
  • Strict Consent Enforcement: Advertising scripts load only when you explicitly grant "Advertising & Monetization" consent via our onsite Cookie Settings center. If consent is rejected, zero ad scripts or network connections execute.
  • Adsterra and its delivery network (bauval.org) process non-personally identifiable technical telemetry (IP address, browser type, device characteristics) to serve contextual display creative. Users can review Adsterra's privacy policy at Adsterra Privacy Policy for Users.
  • Users can opt out of interest-based advertising via the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance at youronlinechoices.eu, the Digital Advertising Alliance at aboutads.info, or the Network Advertising Initiative at networkadvertising.org.

5. User Privacy Controls & Preference Management

You have full control over your privacy settings on PrepWise:

  • Onsite Preference Center: You can review, update, or revoke your consent preferences at any time by clicking the button located in the footer of every page.
  • Browser Storage Clearing: You can delete all local storage and session data by clearing your browser's site data or history for prepwises.online.
  • Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control: We respect browser-level signals requesting minimal tracking.

6. Infrastructure and Security

PrepWise is hosted on Cloudflare Pages global edge infrastructure and powered by Supabase (PostgreSQL). We maintain strict industry-standard security headers:

  • Content-Security-Policy restricting script execution to approved domains and blocking unauthorized injected scripts.
  • X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff preventing MIME-sniffing exploits.
  • Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin protecting user browsing context.
  • frame-ancestors 'none' blocking clickjacking and embedding inside unauthorized third-party frames.

7. Regulatory & Regional Compliance

We design our systems to adhere to global privacy principles:

  • India: Compliant with the principles of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), 2023, by enforcing complete data minimization and zero non-essential personal data collection.
  • United Arab Emirates & GCC: Respects UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45/2021 regarding Personal Data Protection.
  • International (GDPR / CCPA): Because no personal accounts or identifiable data are stored for public students, rights such as data portability and profile erasure are inherently guaranteed by design.
